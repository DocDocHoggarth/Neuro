Spinocerebellar Pathways

Two major 2-neuron ascending pathways carry unconscious proprioceptive and spinal feedback to the cerebellum: the dorsal (posterior) tract stays ipsilateral, while the anterior (ventral) tract decussates twice and still ends functionally ipsilateral overall.

AscendingUnconsciousDorsal = ipsilateralAnterior = double decussation
Pathways at a glance — both are 2-neuron cerebellar relay chains
Peripheral receptors / spinal input→ Spinal cord relay nuclei→ Spinocerebellar tract→ Cerebellar peduncle→ Cerebellum
Dorsal/posterior spinocerebellar tract: typically relays through Clarke's column, stays ipsilateral, and enters through the inferior cerebellar peduncle.
Anterior/ventral spinocerebellar tract: arises mainly from spinal border/intermediate zone neurons, crosses in the spinal cord, ascends, then crosses again as it enters through the superior cerebellar peduncle, so cerebellar input still represents the ipsilateral body overall.
⚠ Neither tract reaches conscious awareness.
